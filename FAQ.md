# FAQ

---

**The game crashes or freezes when I launch / click New Game**  
Make a backup of `Documents\My Games\Oblivion Remastered`, then delete everything inside it.  
This forces the game to regenerate shaders and reset broken config states.

---

**How do I start the main quest?**  
Get arrested by an Imperial Legion guard.  
After the sewer sequence, retrieve your belongings from the evidence chest in the Prison District.

---

**How do I take screenshots?**  
Press **L** to hide the UI, then press **Print Screen**.

---

**Why doesn't the level-up bar show progress in the character menu?**  
This is an issue with the [Mad Experience](https://www.nexusmods.com/oblivionremastered/mods/4881) mod.  
Press **Y** to display a temporary level progress notification in the top-right.

---

**Why am I moving so slowly?**  
Movement speed is affected by:
- Armor weight (heavy armor is slowest)
- Shield usage
- Strafing or moving backwards
- Weapon being drawn

You can adjust these values in:  
`mods\Immersive Movement\UE4SS\ImmersiveMovement\Scripts\main.lua`

---

**How do I open the MCM?**  
Press **F12**

---

**What are `TamrielLeveledRegion.esp` and `AltarGymNagivation.esp`, and why are they disabled?**  
They are leftover base game plugins that ship enabled internally but disabled by default.  
Their function is unclear, and enabling them is not recommended.
