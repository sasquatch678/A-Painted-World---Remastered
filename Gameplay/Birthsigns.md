# <p align="center"> BIRTHSIGNS

All birthsigns now have one passive ability and one lesser power which can be used at will. All of the lesser powers are intended to be powerful but have some kind of penalty, forcing you think about when you use them. Damaged attributes will catch up to you quickly as these effects permanently damage an attribute until you use a restore attribute spell, potion, or pray at a wayshrine of the nine. <br/>

Additionally, there are unique and flavorful greater powers added to the doomstones you can find around the map. These are intended to be very powerful spells you can use once per day, as a sort of ultimate ability you unlock through exploration for each birthsign. You may unlock a doomstone power even if the stone does not match your birthsign choice, but you may only ever have one at a time.
***
## <p align="center"> Combat


***
### Lady
Graceful under pressure and unwavering in spirit, those born under the Lady possess remarkable endurance and poise. Their quiet confidence allows them to weather hardship with ease, though calling upon their greatest gifts demands a measure of fortune in return.

- **Passive:** Graceful Bearing - Gain +35 Fatigue and Restore Fatigue (2/s).</br>
- **Lesser Power:** Lady's Riposte - Gain 25% Reflect Damage and Restore an additional 10 points of Fatigue for 30 seconds, turning your enemies' attacks back upon them at the cost of 3 Luck.</br>
- **Doomstone Power:** Womb of Rebirth - Through the Lady you are given a chance at rebirth, fully restoring all your attributes. However, the process of rebirth leaves you weakened for a time, draining all of your attributes by 25 for 30s.

### Lord 
Those born under the Lord possess an indomitable constitution, enduring wounds that would fell lesser souls. Their fortitude seems almost divine, but even the mightiest champions must lower their guard when calling upon their greatest reserves of vitality.

- **Passive:** Divine Fortitude - Gain +10 Endurance and Restore Health (1/s).</br>
- **Lesser Power:** Lord's Vigour - For 30 seconds, restore 5 Health per second, but suffer 50% Weakness to Magic and 50% Weakness to Normal Weapons for the duration. Great fortitude comes at the cost of leaving yourself dangerously exposed.</br>
- **Doomstone Power:** Rallying Cry - Rally yourself, fortifying endurance by 150 and becoming immune to fear for 120 seconds. However, you become 100% weaker to disease and poison for the duration, the bane of all kings.

### Steed
Restless and ever-moving, those born under the Steed are defined by their boundless energy and relentless momentum. They traverse the world with unmatched swiftness, though such unrestrained speed can leave them briefly unbalanced.

- **Passive:** Fleet Stride - Gain +10 Speed and +10 Athletics.</br>
- **Lesser Power:** Steed's Charge - For 30 seconds, fortify Speed and Athletics by 50 points and gain Water Walking, at the cost of 3 Agility. When the Steed surges forward, few can hope to keep pace, but control is often the price of such overwhelming momentum.</br>
- **Doomstone Power:** Icarian Flight - Call upon the Steed to gallop across the sky, fortifying your speed by 500, acrobatics by 10,000, and granting 25 points of feather for 45s. Hopefully you land before the blessing wanes.

### Warrior
Those born under the Warrior thrive on strength, discipline, and an unyielding will to overcome any foe. They dominate the battlefield through raw might and relentless aggression, breaking enemy ranks with a fearsome battlecry. Yet surrendering to such fury comes at a cost, slowly eroding the clarity of mind needed to master the art of war.

- **Passive:** Martial Discipline - Gain +10 Strength and +10 Shield.</br>
- **Lesser Power:** Warrior's Battlecry - Unleash a fearsome battlecry that causes enemies in the area to take 25% increased damage and become Frenzied for 30 seconds, at the cost of 3 Intelligence. The chaos leaves foes vulnerable to devastating attacks, but the exertion comes at a lasting cost to the Warrior's clarity.</br>
- **Doomstone Power:** Burning Rage - Become an undying whirlwind of flesh and steel, fortifying your strength by 100 points and granting 50% damage reduction for 120 seconds. While enraged you suffer fire damage over time.

## <p align="center"> Magic

***
### Apprentice 
Those born under the Apprentice possess an extraordinary affinity for magic, learning arcane arts with remarkable ease. Yet their eagerness often outpaces their wisdom, and many Apprentices discover that true mastery demands restraint as much as talent.

- **Passive:** Arcane Aptitude - Gain Restore Magicka (2/s) and +10 Willpower.</br>
- **Lesser Power:** Apprentice's Folly - Momentarily abandon caution, regenerating 10 Magicka per second while suffering 50% weakness to magic damage for 30 seconds . The surge of magical energy is immense, but prolonged reliance on such reckless power comes at a price.</br>
- **Doomstone Power:** Arcane Fracture - Unleash a nova of pure arcane energy, causing enemies to be 100% weaker to magic in a 100 yard radius for 60 seconds. However, due to your reckless spellcasting you also become 100% weaker to magic for the duration.

### Atronach 
Those born under the Atronach are vessels of immense magical potential, their bodies attuned to absorb the arcane energies that others wield. Yet this gift comes at a price, severing their connection to the natural flow of Magicka. To sustain their power, they must draw upon the reserves of others, trading their own vitality for the energy they cannot replenish themselves.

- **Passive:** Living Bulwark - Gain 50% Spell Absorption, but suffer from Stunted Magicka, preventing natural Magicka regeneration.</br>
- **Lesser Power:** Atronach's Thirst - Absorb Magicka from nearby enemies, taking their stolen energy to replenish your own reserves. The process consumes your Health, forcing you to sacrifice your vitality to sustain your magic.</br>
- **Doomstone Power:** Null Field - Create a zone of no magicka, silencing, dispelling, and fully depleting magicka of all targets (including yourself) in a 100 yard radius for 60 seconds.

### Mage
Gifted with an innate understanding of the arcane, those born under the Mage wield magic with confidence and clarity. Their brilliance allows them to grasp even the most complex spells, though moments of extraordinary insight can leave their resolve diminished.

- **Passive:** Arcane Mastery - Gain +75 Magicka and +10 Intelligence.</br>
- **Lesser Power:** Mage's Brilliance - For 30 seconds, gain +100 Intelligence at the cost of 3 Willpower. The surge of magical insight greatly enhances your spellcasting, but leaves your mind temporarily less disciplined.</br>
- **Doomstone Power:** Elemental Triune - Conjure a massive storm of elemental power, dealing 10 points of fire, frost, and shock damage in a 75 yard radius for 60 seconds. The storm batters your own defenses, causing you to be 50% weaker to the elements for the duration.

### Ritual 
Those born under the Ritual are bound to the eternal cycle of life and death, wielding sacred power to preserve the living and command the forces that threaten them. Their blessings and curses are two halves of the same divine gift, each demanding a sacrifice of body or spirit.

- **Passive:** Sacred Calling - Gain +10 Restoration, +10 Conjuration, and +10% Magic Resistance.</br>
- **Lesser Powers:** Divine Prayer / Touch of the Damned - Alternate between two lesser powers with each cast. Divine Prayer restores Health and cures poisons and diseases, at the cost of your Magicka. Touch of the Damned absorbs Magicka, traps souls, and dispels magical effects on touch, at the cost of your Health. Life and death are inseparable, and every gift demands its' penance.</br>
- **Doomstone Power:** Profane Ritual - Drain 25 points of your own health for 120s while conjuring a full suit of bound armor, a mace, and a shield, soul trap in an area, and reanimate on touch for the duration of the drain effect.

## <p align="center"> Stealth

***
### Lover 
Blessed with irresistible charm and an effortless gift for persuasion, those born under the Lover excel at winning hearts and defusing conflict. Their presence can soothe even the fiercest tempers, though such enchanting grace leaves them momentarily drained.

- **Passive:** Beguiling Presence - Gain +10 Personality, +10 Speechcraft, and +10 Mercantile.</br>
- **Lesser Power:** Lover's Kiss - Dispel 150 points of magical effects on yourself and on touch, and Calm enemies for 10 seconds, at the cost of 50 Fatigue. Your captivating presence washes away hostility and harmful magic alike, but the effort is physically exhausting.</br>
- **Doomstone Power:** Infatuation - Command any humanoid and restore their health, magicka, and fatigue by 3 for 120s. However when the command ends they will be frenzied for another 30s, attacking anything in sight including you.
  
### Shadow 
Patient and unseen, those born under the Shadow thrive where others would falter. Masters of stealth and deception, they strike from the darkness and vanish before their enemies can react, though every escape comes at the cost of their ability to manipulate others.

- **Passive:** Veiled Step - Gain +10 Agility and +10 Sneak.</br>
- **Lesser Power:** Shadow's Prowl - Become Invisible for 60 seconds and blink to a targeted location, at the cost of 3 Personality. Slip through the darkness unseen, but the strain of bending shadow to your will has a negative effect on your deception and trickery in the future.</br>
- **Doomstone Power:** Aspect of Terror - Demoralize targets in a 100 yard radius causing them to run in fear for 60s. In doing so you lose your ability to sneak, creating a 100 yard area of light centered on yourself for the duration.

### Thief
Those born under the Thief are nimble and dexterous, blessed with uncanny luck and an aptitude for nearly any situation. These cunning rogues excel at moving unseen, outwitting their foes, and pulling off daring heists. Yet even the greatest fortune comes at a price, and every gamble risks tipping the scales.

- **Passive:** Beginner's Luck - Gain +15 Luck.</br>
- **Lesser Power:** Thief's Fate - Fortify Luck, Sneak, and Acrobatics by 50 points and grant Telekinesis at 30 yards for 30 seconds, at the cost of Burden (1000 points) for 5 seconds. Fortune favors the clever, but even the clever must bear its weight.</br>
- **Doomstone Power:** Fortune's Gambit - Gamble with fortune itself, gaining 100% resistance to magic and normal weapons for 60 seconds, but damaging Luck by 3 points. Even the luckiest rogue must pay their dues.

### Tower
Steadfast and immovable, those born under the Tower are natural guardians, gifted with the strength to endure and the vigilance to perceive threats before they strike. Their defenses are formidable, allowing them to weather even the fiercest assaults, though such unwavering resolve comes at the eventual cost of mobility.

- **Passive:** Thorny Aegis - Gain +10% Damage Reflection.</br>
- **Lesser Power:** Tower's Vigilance - Fortify Armor, Resist Magic, and Detect Life by 50 points for 30 seconds, at the cost of 3 points of Speed. Those who stand firm see all, but sacrifice their freedom of movement.</br>
- **Doomstone Power:** Spiked Walls - Surround yourself with an impenetrable barrier of spiked defenses, reflecting 75% of incoming damage for 30 seconds, but paralyzing yourself for 30 seconds. To become an unyielding fortress is to surrender the freedom to move.

## <p align="center"> Other

***
### Serpent
Feared and revered in equal measure, those born under the Serpent walk a treacherous path between blessing and burden. Its gifts sustain the body at the expense of its vigor, while its venom brings even the mightiest foes to their knees. The Serpent offers no mercy, not even to those who bear its mark.

- **Passive:** Betrayer's Debt - Restore Health (2/s), but suffer Damage Fatigue (1/s). The Serpent sustains its bearer through an unnatural bargain, preserving life while slowly sapping their strength.</br>
- **Lesser Power:** Serpent's Bite - Paralyze and absorb fatigue 40 points on touch but lose 30 points of health over 3 seconds. The Serpent's venom can leave even the mightiest foe helpless, but its bite weakens its bearer as well.</br>
- **Doomstone Power:** Star Curse - Curse a target with 100% weakness to poison for 120s and absorb all of their attributes (50 points for 120s), but halt your natural regeneration, damaging your health, magicka, and fatigue by 1/s for 120s.
